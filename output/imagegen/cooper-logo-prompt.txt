Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: product brand identity presentation for a macOS utility named Cooper.
Primary request: Design a beautifully restrained, original logo for Cooper, an app that mounts cloud object storage as normal folders in macOS Finder. The logo idea is a bold geometric letter C fused with an open folder. It should express easy access, connection, and calm reliability.
Symbol: One continuous, substantial, softly rounded C silhouette, squared like a folder, with a subtle raised folder tab at its top-left. The opening on the right is unmistakably the letter C. A small mint rectangular connector sits in the right-hand opening, suggesting a cloud storage volume docking locally. Keep the mark extremely simple, professionally balanced, with generous negative space and no thin lines. No cloud pictogram, arrows, chain links, extra initials, or ornamental elements.
Color palette: Deep navy #15374B, cool mint #67DCC3, warm off-white #F5F7F6. Strong monochrome silhouette. No rainbow or purple gradients.
Composition: A meticulously art-directed landscape brand board. Large symbol and the exact word Cooper in a contemporary rounded sans-serif on the left, on off-white. On the right, one beautiful macOS app icon: navy rounded-square tile with a pale mint version of the same exact symbol, subtle refined depth, front-facing. At the bottom, three tiny monochrome versions show small-size clarity. Plenty of empty space and crisp alignment. No decorative grid or frame. The design is the hero.
Text (verbatim): "Cooper". No other text, no slogan, no labels, no watermark.
Style: Sophisticated independent Mac app identity. Precise vector-like geometry for the logo; only the app tile has subtle dimensional shading. Premium, functional, understated. Do not imitate any existing logo.
